Master's thesis

Weight Transfer for Adversarially Robust Neural Networks via Weight-Space Learning

Visual Computing Group, University of Siegen · Supervisors: Asst. Prof. Dr. Jovita Lukasik and Dr. Marius Bock

Learned PCA and autoencoder operators on permutation-aligned network weights to predict robustness updates for unseen models. The approach recovered up to 69% of the PGD robustness gap without adversarial training and 33% across NAS-Bench-201 families.

An oracle check recovered 91–101% of the gap; adversarial fine-tuning from transferred weights required about 10× fewer epochs. Investigating CIFAR-10 to CIFAR-100 transfer, I found block-wise update similarity decreased from about 0.4 to 0.1, revealing strong dependence on the training data. Thesis grade: 1.7.

ContinuousConvLSTM — generalization across sampling rates
