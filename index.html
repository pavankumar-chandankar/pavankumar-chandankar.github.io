Research and selected projects

My work combines methodological research with reproducible implementations and evaluation pipelines.

M.Sc. thesis

Weight Transfer for Adversarially Robust Neural Networks via Weight-Space Learning

Developed and evaluated PCA- and autoencoder-based weight-space learning approaches to transfer robustness-related information between neural networks. The thesis reported recovery of up to 69% of the PGD robustness gap. Thesis completed; grade 1.7.

Thesis report Repository

Wearable ML

ContinuousConvLSTM: sampling-rate-adaptive activity recognition

Sampling-rate-adaptive temporal convolutions for inertial human activity recognition. The implementation supports rate-specific and multirate training, and leave-one-subject-out experiments on WEAR, RealWorld-HAR, and WISDM-watch. This research underpins the iWOAR 2026 publication.

CodePaper

Sensor ML

Sensor-specific PatchTST ensembles

Designed sensor-specific Transformer models and probability ensembles for wearable activity recognition, including augmentation strategies matched to test conditions. Published in the ACM UbiComp/ISWC 2025 HASCA proceedings.

CodePublication

Wi-Fi CSI

CrossGuard-Fi: reproducible Wi-Fi activity-recognition pipeline

Research-engineering prototype with a 270-channel input specification, subject-disjoint evaluation, recording validation, C++17 feature extraction, PyTorch training, and FastAPI inference. The repository includes software tests and deployment configurations; its synthetic fixtures are not evidence of real-world recognition accuracy.
